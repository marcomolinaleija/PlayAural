\*\*Translations\*\*

Complete a language, correct its texts or propose a new one. You do not need to edit files or know how to program.

\*\*Getting started\*\*

Open Personal and options, Contribute, then Translations. Choose a language. Complete missing texts shows entries that still need work; Review or correct translations includes existing texts.

Choose a section and a text. Search matches the original or translation. Platform and games, Windows, web, Android, and Help and rules have their own sections.

You can translate while seated at a table. Games and voice continue; turns do not pause.

\*\*Writing and trying a translation\*\*

Use arrows to choose, Enter to open and your usual Back control to return. Inside a text box, arrows and Backspace keep their editing functions. Translation menus do not require Tab.

Original opens the full read-only text. Translation opens your draft. Preserve placeholders such as {player}: the game replaces them with names, counts or other data. Gender and quantity variants appear as separate fields.

Drafts save after a brief typing pause and when leaving the field. The example updates privately and does not change anybody else's texts. Preview lets you read the rendered result using example data. Incomplete text can stay in your draft until you fix it before submission.

Starting an edit reserves that text in that language. Reading the original does not. Others see who is working on it without access to the private draft. Reservations expire after 24 hours without editing activity. Release reservation and keep draft makes the text available to someone else without deleting your work.

Save and next opens the next text. Previous opens the preceding text. My proposals resumes your work after reconnecting.

\*\*Submitting and receiving a decision\*\*

Choose Submit for review and confirm. You can also submit the editable drafts on the current My proposals page together. If one has an error, none of that batch is submitted.

Drafts are private. Under review means staff can evaluate the proposal. Withdraw pending work to edit it again. Changes requested includes a reason: correct it and resubmit. Rejected work keeps its text and reason; you can prepare a new proposal from it.

The text stays reserved while under review. Requested changes renew the author's reservation for 24 hours. Approval, rejection and discard release the corresponding reservation.

Approved for the next update means the work was accepted. Your first approval adds you to the language's contributors without granting administrative permissions. The text becomes available with the corresponding release.

You receive a notice when a decision is made and can read it in My proposals. History retains submissions and decisions, including reverted approvals.

If the original changed or another translation was approved, compare the new text and update your reference before resubmitting. Conflicting edits let you read both versions and choose which to keep; your unsaved text is never replaced without confirmation.

\*\*Proposing a language\*\*

Choose Propose a new language in the language list. Enter its standard code, name and native name. Use the existing entry if it is already listed. Its first approved translation confirms the language. It becomes an interface language with its release.

\*\*Reviewing proposals\*\*

Administrators and developers open Administration, then Review translations. Choose a language, a contributor, then a proposal. Each list counts pending work. Compare the original, current translation and proposal. Read its preview and history.

Assigned translators open Personal and options, Contribute, then Review translations. They see and review only their assigned languages. Any approved user may still propose translations; the translator role allows reviewing other people's work without moderation permissions.

Approve accepts work for the next update. Request changes returns it to the author. Reject closes the proposal. The latter two require a reason. Nobody can approve their own work.

After a decision you return to that contributor's next pending proposal. When none remain, you return to the contributor list. Reviewed history uses the same language and contributor grouping.

Reviewed history allows reverting the current approval after confirmation. An older approval cannot overwrite a later decision. Unsubmitted drafts are not available for review.

\*\*Assigning translators\*\*

An administrator or developer opens Administration, then Manage translators. Search for an account, choose a language and confirm to grant or revoke the role. Changes take effect immediately. Revocation keeps their drafts and contributions while removing review access for that language.

The first approved contribution grants contributor credit. The translator role is appointed separately; submitting or approving texts does not grant it automatically.
