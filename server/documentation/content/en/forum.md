\*\*Community Forum\*\*

Choose Forum in the main menu to read and join community discussions. Use your usual PlayAural account on desktop, web, or mobile.

\*\*Finding a Discussion\*\*

Choose Community, Questions and help, or Ideas and feedback. Topics appear first, with their author, message count, activity, and unread status. Actions follow the topics. Recent activity shows discussions across categories. Search looks in titles and visible message text. Saved discussions contains only the discussions you saved.

Saved discussions can include public and private conversations. A private conversation is identified in the list and stays available only while you belong to it. Private conversations do not appear in category lists, Recent activity, Unread discussions, or Search.

On entering a view, a short announcement gives its context. Headings and page summaries do not take up rows. Refresh updates the current list without repeating that introduction or moving focus unnecessarily. Use the page actions at the bottom for longer lists.

\*\*Reading and Continuing\*\*

Open a discussion to find its messages first and its actions below. Open a message to read its full text. Its author and time are announced automatically; the text is split into manageable reading sections.

Opening a full message marks that message and earlier messages in the discussion as read. Reading previews alone does not. Continue from the first unread message and Read the next unread message help you catch up. Your reading position and saved discussions remain available after reconnecting or switching devices.

\*\*Writing\*\*

In a category, choose Start a discussion. Write a title of up to 120 characters and a message of up to 8000 characters. In an existing discussion, choose Write a reply. Submitting the text returns to the form; choose Publish to send it. Your text is plain text, so HTML and Markdown commands are not executed.

Each field shows its label and current content in one editable item: Title and Message. Replies show only Message. Long messages show an excerpt; open that same field to review and edit the full text. Actions follow the fields. Back asks before discarding a draft containing text. Drafts stay in the current session; text already submitted to the form can survive a brief reconnect or device handover, but it is not saved permanently. Text still being typed has not reached the server.

You may edit your visible messages for 30 minutes. If someone else changed the same message, publishing your older edit is rejected and your draft remains available. Closed discussions do not accept replies or ordinary author edits. Sending too many messages in a short time also keeps your draft for later review.

\*\*Files, Images, Audio, and Video\*\*

While composing a discussion or reply, choose Add, then Attach a file. This action appears when the server supports attachments. Choose a file from your device. Wait for its status to change from uploading or preparing to ready, then open it from the form to check it. You can add a description or remove it before publishing. Removing it from the draft does not delete the original file on your device.

An image opens in a viewer with zoom and its description. Audio and video open in a player inside PlayAural; choose Play to start. Use the playback controls to pause, seek, change speed or volume, and close the player. Videos also offer full screen. Opening a file never starts playback automatically. During playback, PlayAural lowers background music and ambience temporarily. Closing returns you to the forum item you opened.

Save file lets you keep a copy on your device; on mobile, choose a destination using the system's save or share options. PDF, ZIP, and plain text files are downloads. You can attach JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP images, MP3, WAV, FLAC, or Ogg audio, and MP4 or WebM media. Images are prepared as still images. Audio and video may be converted before publication, and the prepared filename may change.

Each file can be at most 128 MiB. Audio and video can last up to 15 minutes, and images can contain up to 40 million pixels. You can keep up to eight unpublished files totalling 256 MiB. Unpublished uploads expire after 24 hours, including files awaiting approval; upload them again if they expire. A failed or rejected file must be removed or replaced before publishing.

If attachment approval is enabled, choosing Publish submits your ready files for review and keeps the message as a draft. Public files are reviewed by community moderators; files in an existing private conversation are reviewed by its owner. They cannot inspect files you have not submitted for review. When approved, return to the form and choose Publish again. Approval alone does not publish your message. Owners' own private files and moderators' own public files do not need this review.

Video subtitles can be preserved when the uploaded video contains a supported text subtitle track. Use the player's subtitle control when one is available. PlayAural does not generate subtitles automatically; separate subtitle uploads are not linked to videos.

\*\*Polls\*\*

While composing, choose Add, then Add a poll. Enter a question of up to 240 characters and two to ten different answers, one per line, each up to 160 characters. By default, people choose one answer, can change their vote, see results after voting, and have no automatic deadline. You can allow multiple answers, prevent vote changes, show results immediately or after closing, and set a deadline of 1, 3, 7, or 30 days. Check the question and answers in the message form, then publish.

Open a message's poll, choose your answers, and select Submit vote to confirm. Selecting an answer only changes your pending selection. Back leaves without submitting it. If vote changes are allowed, submit a new selection to replace your vote. Results appear according to the poll's setting; they count votes without listing voters' names. The author or conversation moderator can close a poll after confirmation. A closed conversation also prevents voting. Questions and answers cannot change after the first vote.

\*\*Private Conversations\*\*

Choose Private conversations, then Start a private conversation. Enter the usernames you want to invite, one per line, and prepare a title and message as usual. Invitations are sent only when you publish. A conversation can hold up to 12 people, including its owner and pending invitations.

In Private conversations, open Pending invitations to accept or decline. Before accepting, you see the title and who invited you. Accepting opens the conversation and grants access to its earlier messages and files. Invitations expire after seven days. Administrators also need an accepted invitation to read private conversations.

Open Participants to see who belongs to the conversation. The owner can invite people or select a participant to remove them, after confirmation. Leave this conversation asks before ending your access. Leaving or being removed also ends access to its files and removes it from your saved discussions. You need another invitation to return. If the owner leaves, ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member. If nobody remains, the conversation is deleted. Already downloaded copies remain on their recipients' devices.

\*\*Community Controls\*\*

Approval, bans, mutes, and user blocks also apply in the forum. A block in either direction hides the other account's public discussions and messages in new views and prevents new private invitations between those accounts. It does not erase text already received or remove existing group members; other participants can continue the conversation. Administrators and developers can close or reopen public discussions, pin them, and hide or restore individual messages after confirmation. Private conversation owners manage those controls in their own conversations.

Public discussion text remains after account deletion, with the deleted author's attribution removed. Personal saved discussions and reading positions are deleted with the account.

\*\*Navigation\*\*

Use the usual keyboard, touch, screen reader, or self-voicing controls. Actions are at the bottom, and Back returns to the item that opened the view when it is still available. Escape cancels confirmation prompts. On mobile, Back closes an open media player before leaving the underlying forum view.

\*\*Manage subforums\*\*

Administrators and developers have a Manage subforums action at the bottom of the forum. Choose Create subforum or open an existing one to change its name and description. Names allow up to 80 characters and descriptions up to 500; leave a description empty to remove it. Changes take effect when you choose Save subforum. Up to 64 subforums are supported.

Descriptions are announced on entering a subforum without adding a row before its topics. People already viewing the lists receive updates while keeping focus when their selected item remains available.

Delete subforum asks for confirmation. If it contains topics, first choose another subforum to keep them; messages, files, polls, bookmarks and reading positions are preserved. Create another subforum first if none is available. Private conversations remain separate and do not appear in this management view. If another person changed a subforum while you were editing, your draft is kept and you are asked to reopen it instead of overwriting their changes.
