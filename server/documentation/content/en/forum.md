\*\*Community Forum\*\*

Choose Forum in the main menu to read and join community discussions. Use your usual PlayAural account on desktop, web, or mobile.

\*\*Finding a Discussion\*\*

Choose Community, Questions and help, or Ideas and feedback. Topics appear first, with their author, message count, activity, and unread status. Actions follow the topics. Recent activity shows discussions across categories. Search looks in titles and visible message text. Saved discussions contains only the discussions you saved.

On entering a view, a short announcement gives its context. Headings and page summaries do not take up rows. Refresh updates the current list without repeating that introduction or moving focus unnecessarily. Use the page actions at the bottom for longer lists.

\*\*Reading and Continuing\*\*

Open a discussion to find its messages first and its actions below. Open a message to read its full text. Its author and time are announced automatically; the text is split into manageable reading sections.

Opening a full message marks that message and earlier messages in the discussion as read. Reading previews alone does not. Continue from the first unread message and Read the next unread message help you catch up. Your reading position and saved discussions remain available after reconnecting or switching devices.

\*\*Writing\*\*

In a category, choose Start a discussion. Write a title of up to 120 characters and a message of up to 8000 characters. In an existing discussion, choose Write a reply. Submitting the text opens its preview; choose Publish to send it. Your text is plain text, so HTML and Markdown commands are not executed.

The preview appears before its editing and publishing actions. Back asks before discarding a draft containing text. Drafts stay in the current session; text already submitted for preview can survive a brief reconnect or device handover, but it is not saved permanently. Text still being typed has not reached the server.

You may edit your visible messages for 30 minutes. If someone else changed the same message, publishing your older edit is rejected and your draft remains available. Closed discussions do not accept replies or ordinary author edits. Sending too many messages in a short time also keeps your draft for later review.

\*\*Community Controls\*\*

Approval, bans, mutes, and user blocks also apply in the forum. A block in either direction hides the other account's discussions and messages in new views. It does not erase text already received. Administrators and developers can close or reopen discussions, pin them, and hide or restore individual messages after confirmation.

Public discussion text remains after account deletion, with the deleted author's attribution removed. Personal saved discussions and reading positions are deleted with the account.

\*\*Navigation\*\*

Use the usual keyboard, touch, screen reader, or self-voicing controls. Actions are at the bottom, and Back returns to the item that opened the view when it is still available. Escape cancels confirmation prompts. This first version supports text discussions; attachments, polls, private forum threads, and mentions are not yet available.
