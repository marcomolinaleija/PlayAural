\*\*Mexipoly\*\*

\*\*Overview and goal\*\*

Mexipoly is a property-trading game for 2 to 8 players on a 50-space board. Buy cities, collect rent, complete regional groups, and build houses and hotels. The last player who can still meet their debts wins. There are no points or target score.

Choose Mexipoly as its own game in the Play menu. It is not the 40-space Mexico board available inside Monopoly. Both games keep separate saved games, statistics, and Brief announcements preferences.

PlayAural manages the Bank, dice, movement, cards, auctions, and calculations. Cash, positions, property ownership, mortgages, houses, hotels, and jail cards are public. Trade drafts remain private until submitted.

\*\*Starting and the larger board\*\*

Create a table and add at least one opponent; bots can fill seats, but at least one active human must participate. The host chooses the options before starting. New tables use the 50-space board; saved matches retain their original board size and ownership.

Everyone starts at Go with 3,000 Mexican pesos. Passing or landing on Go pays 400. The Bank has 64 houses and 24 hotels. Opening rolls choose the first player, with ties rerolled. Wait for those rolls to finish before acting.

The default board has 24 cities in eight colors, four metro stations, four airports, two utilities, five Chance spaces, five Community Chest spaces, two taxes, Go, Jail, Free Parking, and Go to Jail. Own the three cities of one color to build. The cities are grouped geographically, but the color names stay simple:

\* brown color group: Tijuana, Mexicali, La Paz.
\* light blue color group: Hermosillo, Chihuahua, Ciudad Juárez.
\* pink color group: Saltillo, Monterrey, Tampico.
\* orange color group: Zacatecas, Aguascalientes, San Luis Potosí.
\* red color group: Guadalajara, León, Morelia.
\* yellow color group: Querétaro, Toluca, Ciudad de México.
\* green color group: Puebla, Veracruz, Oaxaca de Juárez.
\* dark blue color group: Mérida, Cancún, Chetumal.

These are broad geographic groupings, not connected metro lines or a road itinerary. Prices are game values, not real land valuations. Four stations come from Metrorrey in Monterrey, SITEUR in the Guadalajara metropolitan area, and the Mexico City Metro. The airports are Tijuana, Monterrey in Apodaca, Benito Juárez in Mexico City, and Cancún. Airports and metro stations are separate ownership groups.

\*\*A turn, step by step\*\*

1. Before rolling, manage your properties or propose trades. In jail, choose to pay the fine (100 pesos the first time, doubling successively on each re-entry: 200, 400...), use a Get Out of Jail Free card, or roll the dice directly using the standard Roll dice button.
2. Choose Roll dice. Add two six-sided dice and move that many spaces.
3. Finish the landing: purchases, auctions, rent, cards, and any debt must be resolved first.
4. A non-double then ends the turn automatically. Doubles keep the turn and require another roll with the standard Roll dice button; you may manage assets before that roll. Three consecutive doubles send you to jail. Doubles used to leave jail do not give another roll.

Another player may need to respond during your turn, especially to claim or waive rent, bid, or answer a trade. Check whose turn identifies both the turn player and the required responder. Information actions remain available while sounds or movement resolve.

\*\*Buying, auctions, and rent\*\*

A property menu shows the price, mortgage value, region members, development cost, rent schedule, and your cash. Buying pays the Bank and gives you the deed. When auctions are enabled (default), declining or being unable to buy starts a live auction for every eligible player, including you; when disabled, the property stays with the Bank and the turn continues. In an auction, the opening bid is 10 pesos and each raise is at least 10. Any active bidder may place a bid or leave the auction at any time while the gavel counts "going once", "going twice", and "going three times" at the configured interval; every new bid resets the countdown. If nobody bids after the third call or everyone leaves without bidding, the Bank keeps the deed; otherwise the last highest bidder wins it.

Automatic rent is On by default: landing on another owner's unmortgaged property charges rent without asking the owner. Utilities still require their fresh dice roll. If the tenant cannot pay, the debt remains open for liquidation; the turn does not advance prematurely. Turn this option Off to let owners choose Claim rent or Waive rent. You never pay rent to yourself or on mortgaged property.

\* \*\*Cities:\*\* Use their deed's rent schedule (with prices from 160 to 1280 pesos). Owning 2 of the 3 cities in a color group without buildings increases base rent by 50%; owning all three cities in a full unbuilt color group multiplies base rent by 2.5 and permits construction.
\* \*\*Metro stations:\*\* Each station costs 400 and mortgages for 200. Owning 1 through 4 stations gives rent of 60, 120, 240, or 480 pesos.
\* \*\*Airports:\*\* Each airport costs 500 and mortgages for 250. Owning 1 through 4 airports gives rent of 80, 160, 320, or 640 pesos. Airport ownership never raises metro rent, or vice versa.
\* \*\*Utilities:\*\* CFE and CONAGUA cost 360 each (mortgage 180) and use a fresh dice roll. Pay 10 times its total for one utility or 24 times for both.

The deed always shows the relevant schedule.

\*\*Construction, mortgages, and trades\*\*

Own all three cities of a region, with none mortgaged, before building. Build evenly: all three need one house before any can have two. Five houses form a hotel. Houses cost the city's building rate (from 120 to 800 pesos) and hotels carry a 50 percent cost premium (from 180 to 1200 pesos). Depending on the location and its metropolitan or tourist importance (such as Cancún, Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, and Tijuana), a property permits up to two hotels, as specified on its deed. Bank supplies are finite (64 houses and 24 hotels). Information panels show remaining houses and hotels.

Manage properties lets you choose what to build, sell, mortgage, or unmortgage. Buildings sell for half their purchase cost, following even-selling rules. Remove all buildings in a region before mortgaging a city. Mortgaged property stays yours but earns no rent. Unmortgaging costs the mortgage principal plus 10 percent interest. A recipient of mortgaged property must also resolve the transfer interest.

Propose a trade to offer or request cash, eligible properties, and jail cards. The other player must accept before assets change hands. Cities with development cannot be traded until their region's development is sold. Cancelled or rejected trades transfer nothing.

\*\*Mexican folklore cards, SAT audit, and protection racket (cobro de piso)\*\*

Chance has 26 cards and Community Chest has 32 cards inspired by Mexican folklore (office savings pools, Rosca de Reyes and Candelaria tamales, quinceañera sponsorships, El Torito breathalyzer checkpoints, CFE meter wiretaps, giant potholes, remittances, speed cameras, trips to Cancún, mariachis in Guadalajara, Northern barbecues in Monterrey, the Guelaguetza in Oaxaca, paperwork in Mexico City, port Carnival in Veracruz, connecting flights, SAT tax refunds, and false harassment complaints). Read the announced instruction: collect or pay cash, move to a named city or transport hub, go back, pay repairs or property tax, or enter or leave jail. Movement instructions use this 50-space board. A card says whether passing Go pays the salary. Nearest-transport cards can select a station or airport and double its usual rent; nearest-utility cards use a fresh roll at the ten-times rate. Used cards return to their deck except held jail cards.

\* \*\*SAT tax-evasion audit:\*\* Drawing the SAT audit card charges 20 percent of your current cash (minimum 300 pesos) and sends you to jail for 2 mandatory turns with no bail, no Get Out of Jail Free card usage, and no rent collection on your properties while held by the SAT.
\* \*\*Protection racket (cobro de piso) and Mexican Navy raids:\*\* Either randomly at the start of a turn or through Chance and Community Chest cards, organized crime can activate the protection racket across the board. While active, every rent collected suffers a deduction by property development level: 5 percent on normal properties (undeveloped cities, metro stations, airports, and utilities), 10 percent with 1 or 2 houses, 15 percent with 3 to 5 houses, 30 percent with 1 hotel, and 50 percent at maximum hotels. The protection racket lasts at least 8 turns before a random Mexican Navy (Marina) operation can end it (or ends immediately when a Marina card is drawn). Check Read game status (key E) at any time to see whether the protection racket is active.
\* \*\*Property vandalism and suspect accusation:\*\* Once per turn before rolling (or before an extra doubles roll), you may pay to secretly vandalize an unmortgaged opponent property (key X). A vandalized property collects only half rent and cannot build new developments for 3 turns. The targeted owner may either denounce who they believe did it or let it go: if they guess the culprit, the vandal pays proportional damages to the owner and the damage is repaired immediately; if they make a false accusation, the accuser pays a proportional penalty to the falsely accused player and the property stays vandalized.
\* \*\*Special board events:\*\* Temporary 6-turn board events can trigger during play, including Tourism festival (+50 percent rent in one color group), Smog alert & Hoy No Circula (+50 percent Metro and Airport rent), and National blackout (+50 percent CFE and CONAGUA rent).

SAT tax spaces charge 450 pesos for income tax and 250 for luxury tax (25 percent higher in Crisis mode). Free Parking pays nothing unless its optional jackpot is enabled. Landing normally on Jail is only a visit. Go to Jail moves directly to space 14, collects no Go salary, and ends the turn. Leaving regular jail costs 100 pesos the first time, 200 the second, 400 the third, and doubles on each subsequent imprisonment. After the third failed regular jail roll, pay the accumulated fine and move by that roll.

If a debt exceeds your cash, sell legal buildings or mortgage property. If your total liquid assets cannot cover what you owe, you automatically declare bankruptcy to keep the game flowing fast. If you still have liquidable assets, Automatically raise cash can do this for you or Manage properties lets you choose. Assets go to the creditor or Bank according to the debt. Having no cash by itself does not end your game.

\*\*Options\*\*

\* \*\*Economic mode:\*\* Normal (3,000 start and 400 Go salary), Crisis & inflation (2,400 start, 300 Go salary, taxes +25 percent, and longer protection racket), Economic boom (4,000 start and 550 Go salary), or Mexican chaos (3,200 start, 450 Go salary, and double special event frequency). \*\*Default: Normal.\*\*
\* \*\*Automatic rent:\*\* Collects rent on landing; turn Off for manual collection or waiver. \*\*Default: On.\*\*
\* \*\*Auctions when declining purchase:\*\* When On, declining a purchase opens a live gavel auction ("going once, going twice, going three times!"); when Off, the property stays with the Bank and the turn continues. \*\*Default: On.\*\*
\* \*\*Auction gavel interval:\*\* 3, 5, 8, 10, or 15 seconds between each gavel call during an auction. \*\*Default: 5 seconds.\*\*
\* \*\*Turn time:\*\* Unlimited, 20, 30, or 40 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes. Limits the whole turn, without resetting when opening property management. Audio resolutions pause it; other required players have their own response budget. On timeout, roll automatically, decline a purchase into auction (or pass without buying if auctions are disabled), leave bidding, close management, cancel or reject a trade, or settle a debt using available assets. No automatic trade acceptance or property purchase. \*\*Default: Unlimited.\*\*

\* \*\*Rest-space jackpot:\*\* Taxes, fines, and repairs form a pot collected at Free Parking. \*\*Default: Off.\*\*
\* \*\*Double salary on Go:\*\* Landing exactly on Go pays 800; merely passing pays 400. \*\*Default: Off.\*\*
\* \*\*No rent while in jail:\*\* An owner in jail cannot collect rent. \*\*Default: Off.\*\*
\* \*\*Pass Go before buying:\*\* You must reach or pass Go once before buying or bidding. \*\*Default: Off.\*\*
\* \*\*Snake eyes bonus:\*\* Rolling two ones gives 500 pesos, also when rolling in jail. \*\*Default: Off.\*\*

\*\*Information, accessibility, and shortcuts\*\*

Use Read board to explore all 50 spaces and their stable rows. Read property groups lists the regions and transport groups. Read cash, Read your portfolio, Read all portfolios, and Read game status explain your position, resources, and required decisions. Touch clients expose these through the menus; spectators can inspect public state without changing it.

Space rolls dice; F reads your space; V reads the board; C reads cash; Shift+C reads groups; P reads your portfolio; O reads all portfolios; E reads status; G manages properties; R proposes a trade; X vandalizes an opponent property; T checks whose turn; Ctrl+U lists table participants. F1 describes the focused menu item, Ctrl+F1 opens this guide, and Escape opens the actions menu. Framework-owned exits restore focus to their opener where possible.
