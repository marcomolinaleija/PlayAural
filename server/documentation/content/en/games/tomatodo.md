**Toma Todo (Mexican Pirinola)**

**What is Toma Todo?**

Toma Todo is the traditional Mexican spinning top (pirinola) game, adapted for PlayAural using platform virtual wallet coins. It is a fast-paced and thrilling game for 2 to 8 players where the outcome of each spin can award you the entire pot or send you into bankruptcy!

**Game Rules**

When the game begins, every player contributes an initial ante to the central pot using coins from their platform wallet. Players take turns spinning the six-sided pirinola. When it stops, the top face determines the required action:

* **Put 1 (Pon 1):** The active player places 1 unit into the pot.
* **Put 2 (Pon 2):** The active player places 2 units into the pot.
* **Take 1 (Toma 1):** The active player takes 1 unit from the pot.
* **Take 2 (Toma 2):** The active player takes 2 units from the pot.
* **Everyone Puts (Todos ponen):** Every active player puts 1 unit into the pot!
* **Take All (Toma todo):** The active player claims the entire pot!

**Pot Replenishment & Bankruptcy**

* Whenever the pot is emptied (either via Take All or by taking the last coins via Take 1 or 2), all active players contribute 1 unit to replenish the pot.
* If a player's coins reach zero and they cannot pay an ante or required penalty, they go **bankrupt** and are eliminated from active play.
* The last remaining player with coins is crowned the winner and collects any leftover coins in the pot.

**Table Options**

* **Coin unit:** Sets the coin value of each unit (1, 5, 10, 25, or 50 coins).
* **Initial ante:** Sets whether each player contributes 1 or 2 units at the start.
* **Round limit:** Choose between continuous play (until only one player remains) or a set limit of 10, 15, or 20 rounds where the player with the most coins wins.

**Controls and Shortcuts**

* **Space / Enter / G:** Spin the pirinola on your turn.
* **Standard actions menu:** Check the current pot, check coin balances of all players, and check the last spin result.
