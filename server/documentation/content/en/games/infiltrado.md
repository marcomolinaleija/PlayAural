**Undercover (The Impostor)**

**What is Undercover?**

Undercover is a multiplayer social deduction game of secret words, bluffing, and hidden identities for 3 to 12 players. At the start of the match, each player receives a secret word. Most players share the exact same civilian word, but hidden among them are one or more undercovers with a slightly different word... and possibly someone with no word at all!

The game can be enjoyed with both real-time voice chat and written text.

**Roles**

* **Civilians:** The majority of players. Everyone in this group receives the exact same secret word (e.g. "Coffee"). Their objective is to deduce who the undercovers are by listening to clues and vote them out.
* **Undercovers:** Receive a word closely related to the civilian word, but subtly different (e.g. "Tea"). At the start, players do not know whether they are a civilian or an undercover; they must listen closely to other clues, adapt, and blend in. Their objective is to survive until they equal or outnumber the civilians.
* **Mr. White (optional):** Receives no word at all. Mr. White must listen carefully to deduce what topic is being discussed and bluff with a convincing clue. If voted out during elimination, Mr. White gets one final golden opportunity: guessing the exact civilian word to snatch victory!

**Game Phases**

**1. Clues Phase**
In turn order, each player gives a clue about their secret word:
* The clue should be a single word or short phrase. You cannot say your secret word directly.
* **Voice mode:** Speak your clue over the microphone and press **Space** ("Word said") to pass the turn to the next player.
* **Text and voice mode:** You can type your clue in an edit box (which saves it to the clues history for review at any time) or say it by voice.
* You can configure 1 to 5 clue rounds before moving to the voting phase.

**2. Voting Phase**
After clue rounds are complete, players debate and vote on whom to eliminate:
* Each participant casts a vote for the player they find most suspicious.
* The player with the most votes is eliminated.
* In case of a tie, no one is eliminated and a new clue round begins.

**3. Mr. White Guess**
If Mr. White is eliminated by vote:
* A special deduction turn begins.
* Mr. White has a limited time to guess the civilian secret word. A correct guess wins the game instantly!

**Victory Conditions**

* **Civilians win:** When all Undercovers and Mr. White are eliminated (without Mr. White correctly guessing the civilian word).
* **Undercovers win:** When undercovers equal or outnumber the living civilians.
* **Mr. White wins:** By correctly guessing the civilian word upon elimination, or by surviving to the end.

**Table Options**

* **Clue mode:** Voice only or Text and voice.
* **Word theme:** Play with a specific theme (Food and drink, Animals, Places, Objects, etc.) or with All themes.
* **Undercover count:** 1 or 2 undercovers (requires at least 5 players for 2 undercovers).
* **Include Mr. White:** Yes or No (requires at least 4 players).
* **Clue rounds before vote:** 1 to 5 complete rounds of clues before voting.
* **Turn timer:** Time limit per clue (No limit, 30, 45, or 60 seconds).
* **Vote timer:** Time limit for debate and voting (30, 45, 60, or 90 seconds).

**Controls and Shortcuts**

* **Alt+W:** Check your secret word and role.
* **Space:** Confirm clue given ("Word said").
* **H:** View clue history.
* **T:** Announce whose turn it is and remaining time.
* **Ctrl+I:** View game information and active options.
* **Ctrl+F1:** Open this How to Play guide.
