**Mexican Loteria**

**What is Mexican Loteria?**

Mexican Loteria is a traditional party game for 2 to 12 players. Each participant plays with a 4x4 board (16 cards) drawn from the traditional 54-card Mexican deck. The caller ("El Gritón") draws one card at a time at a configurable interval.

As cards are called, players place beans on matching pictures on their board, racing to complete the winning pattern chosen by the host. There are no individual turns: all players follow the calls and mark their boards simultaneously.

**How to Win**

Complete the designated winning pattern using cards that have genuinely been called from the deck, then choose **Call Loteria!** (or press B). The game verifies your board following a drumroll suspense beat before confirming your victory.

Completing the pattern silently does not win the round: you must call Loteria while the round remains active. The first player to submit a valid claim wins the match.

**Your Loteria Board**

Your board features 16 distinct cards arranged in 4 rows and 4 columns.

Navigating the grid with arrow keys or keypad announces the card name and whether a bean has been placed. For example: "The Umbrella, marked with bean" or "The Mermaid, unmarked."

Press Enter on any cell to place or remove a bean.

**Winning Patterns**

The host can configure any of the classic patterns:

* **Any line:** Four marked cards along any horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal.
* **Four corners:** The four corner cells of your board.
* **Center square:** The 2x2 square in the center of the board.
* **Small square:** Any 2x2 square anywhere on the board.
* **Full board:** All 16 cells marked.

**Controls and Shortcuts**

* **Arrow keys:** Navigate your 4x4 board.
* **Enter:** Place or remove a bean on the focused card.
* **B:** Call Loteria / Buenas (on touch clients, Shift+Enter or the action item).
* **R:** Repeat the last called card.
* **C:** Check the list of called cards.
* **T:** Announce the time remaining until the next card or current round state.
